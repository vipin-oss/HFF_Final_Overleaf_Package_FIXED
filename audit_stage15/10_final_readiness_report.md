## 5. The decision that is being asked for

**RU-01.** A9 specifies a *rigid* casing whose interior pressure equals the outlet datum. Under the
convention that the evidence requires, the strip bows **toward** that casing by `0.219 mm`. The
source never states a standoff distance. The three readings are:

- **`R1`** vented cavity, standoff `> 0.4377 H₀` — **every number in the manuscript stands**;
- **`R2`** sealed cavity — back pressure rises with the bow; `w_max`, `Γ_p` and the stresses change;
- **`R3`** contact / standoff `< w_max` — the bow is blocked; the frozen-force beam–column solution
  is **invalid**, not merely approximate.

`R1` is the reading the text leans towards (`ch04:287`, "casing at the same reference pressure") but
it is inferred, not stated. **This was not resolved by adopting `R1`**, precisely because `R1` is the
reading that preserves the numbers — which is the test the protocol forbids.

**Minimum answer needed:** the clearance behind the heated face, and whether that cavity is vented
to the outlet or sealed.

**RU-03 (secondary):** state the deformed mid-span height. Under the adopted convention it is
`1.4377 H₀` — the passage widens. No such sentence exists anywhere in the source.

## 6. What can proceed now

Coding may begin on the four components that do not depend on RU-01:

1. fluid solver, verified against V-A (plane Poiseuille) and V-B (rigid conjugate thermal);
2. conjugate thermal solver, `P2` with SUPG `eq:supg`;
3. thermoelastic solver, verified against V-C1 and V-C2 (free strip; `σ_m = −Eαθ/3`, and
   `κ = (1+ν)αΔT_t/d > 0` with the hot face convex);
4. beam–column verification against V-C3 (`w_max = κ_thL²/8 > 0`, away from the channel).

Coding must **not** begin on coupled production runs, baseline mesh convergence, or any headline
`Γ_p`/stress figure until RU-01 is answered.
